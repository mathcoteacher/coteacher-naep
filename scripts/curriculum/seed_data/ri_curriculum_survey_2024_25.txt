This data was collected from all Rhode Island LEAs at the start of the 2024-2025 school year. The colors are based on the EdReports rating system: Green=Meet Expectations,
Yellow= Partially Meet Expectations, Red= Do Not Meet. For RIDE purposes, we have added: Purple=Not Yet Rated, Blue=Locally Developed (written by teachers and/or multiple
curricula). EdReports is an independent non-profit designed to improve K-12 education by reviewing curriculum materials (www.edreports.org). For high schools, the
mathematics information reflects materials for Algebra 1, Geometry, and Algebra 2.                                                                                   *Latest
